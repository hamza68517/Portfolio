I'm Hamza Ali — a full-stack web developer and WordPress specialist. I help founders, agencies, and small teams ship websites that look sharp, load fast, and actually move the needle.

My day-to-day is a mix of custom WordPress builds, PHP templates, and modern frontend work with JavaScript, React, and Tailwind CSS. I like living on both sides of the stack — the layout that a visitor sees and the logic that quietly keeps everything running behind it.

I care about the small things: a clear layout, a sensible content model, a hover state that feels right. That's usually what separates a website that works from one that just looks nice.

Design-aware development
